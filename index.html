Client Onboarding, Reimagined

A global investment bank's Prime Brokerage division onboards hedge funds and asset managers through a multi-month process spanning Sales, Credit Risk, KYC, Legal, and Connectivity teams. I mapped how that process actually felt from the inside, then storyboarded a connected system to fix it.

CONTEXT & ROLE

Why this mattered

Every new client meant a slow, manual re-run of the same onboarding steps across disconnected teams. Clients felt it as confusion and delay; internally, it meant duplicated work and no shared view of progress. My job was to make the problem visible, then design what "fixed" could look like.

  • Research design & fieldwork

    Planned and ran interviews with clients and internal stakeholders across Sales, Transitions, Credit, KYC, Legal and Connectivity.

  • Service blueprint authorship

    Synthesised interviews into a detailed as-is blueprint, mapping front-stage interactions against back-stage systems and an emotional journey curve.

  • Concept design & storyboarding

    Led the to-be concept direction and storyboarded it scene-by-scene so both client-facing and internal teams could see how it would feel in practice.

  • Stakeholder alignment

    Helped present the finished storyboard back to client and internal stakeholders — using it to build alignment around the direction, not just explain it.

AS-IS · WHAT WE FOUND

A journey that felt fragmented from the inside

Across dozens of client and internal interviews, the same shape of complaint kept surfacing: nobody — client or bank — had one place to see where things stood.

No visibility

"We can't see the overall progress."

Clients had no shared view of where their case stood, or what was actually urgent versus informational.

Repetition

Asked for the same information twice.

Teams operated on separate document trails — nothing carried over from Sales to KYC to Legal.

No prioritization

Everything arrived marked urgent.

50+ documents could land at once with no signal on what was blocking versus simply informational.

Fragmented ownership

"Feels like two different companies."

KYC and onboarding teams didn't share context — clients felt handed between firms, not departments.

Late signal

Account numbers arrived too late to test.

Clients couldn't start their own technical setup until deep into the process, compressing the runway to launch.

Inconsistent formats

Every team reported differently.

Inconsistent naming conventions across reports meant manual rework even after go-live.

TO-BE · THE SHIFT

One shared system, not five separate ones

Almost every fix traces back to the same move: replace scattered emails, calls and spreadsheets with one place both the client and the bank's teams can see and act on.

01

Progressive profile, not repeated intake

Information is captured once, as early as the first sales conversation, and carried forward automatically.

02

Shared timeline over status calls

A visible tracker replaces "where are we?" calls — everyone sees the same plan, the same dates.

03

Guided, not generic, requests

Every ask tells the client why it's needed and what's still outstanding — no more 50-document dumps with no context.

OUTCOME

What this made possible

The storyboard gave both the client-facing and internal teams a shared, concrete picture of what "better" looked like — not just a wish list, but a scene-by-scene walkthrough they could react to, critique, and build against. It reframed onboarding from a series of disconnected handoffs into one continuous, visible relationship.
